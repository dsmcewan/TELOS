# Decision AM-43 — classify `cli/` and `connectors/meta-ads-mcp/` as excluded package dirs (PYLAE Gate v1)

- **what** — Under the product-1 quest (v0.3.0 / PYLAE Gate v1), the two new
  directories `cli/` (the pylae CLI) and `connectors/meta-ads-mcp/` are
  classified into `PACKAGE_ROOTS_EXCLUDE`. This AMENDS AM-42's closed set
  (AM-42's bytes are never rewritten; its seven-package ruling remains the true
  record of what was ruled then). `PACKAGE_ROOTS` is UNCHANGED — no new spine
  package is enrolled; only the exclusion set grows by the two product/connector
  dirs. The closed membership after activation is machine-bound to the single
  fenced JSON block below (verify-contracts parses it and requires the contract
  arrays to deep-equal it once package-roots.json is repinned in Phase B):

```json
{
  "package_roots": ["atropos", "breakout", "build-gate", "clotho", "connectors/ai-peer-mcp", "lachesis", "merkle-dag"],
  "package_roots_exclude": ["ai-forge", "ai-native-memory", "cli", "connectors/meta-ads-mcp", "demo", "forge", "narcissus/flagship", "saas-forge"]
}
```

- **why** — `cli/` and `connectors/meta-ads-mcp/` are product/connector surfaces
  shipped by the product-1 quest, not TELOS spine packages. They must be
  excluded from the spine inventory (and its exact-bijection gates) exactly as
  the other non-spine products (`narcissus/flagship`, `ai-native-memory`, `demo`,
  the forges) are. Recording this as a hash-pinned ruling binds BOTH arrays so
  neither the roots nor the exclusions can drift when the two dirs land.

- **two-phase lifecycle (DISCOVERY-001 ruling)** — this decision is AUTHORIZED
  at Phase A and ACTIVATED ATOMICALLY IN PHASE B with the directories it governs.
  Pinning AM-43 into `package-roots.json` at the Phase A head — while `cli/` and
  `connectors/meta-ads-mcp/` do not yet exist and `inventory.mjs`/`test-inventory`
  are deferred — would break the repository's own exact-bijection inventory gates
  at that head. Therefore Phase A STAGES this doc in its non-woven location
  (`telos/AMENDMENTS/AM-43.md`) and records the council authorization + the
  `amendments_in_force` registry row marked `activation: "deferred-to-phase-b"`,
  with `package-roots.json` byte-unchanged on its AM-42 pin. Phase B's atomic
  activation slice then: canonicalizes this doc into `clotho/memory/DECISIONS/`
  (byte-identical move, sha unchanged), repins
  `package-roots.json.authority.enrollment_ruling` to AM-43's sha, adds both
  directories, and updates `inventory.mjs` + `test-inventory` + the coordinated
  surfaces — all under the newly-landed discriminating verifier.

- **scope** — the closed `PACKAGE_ROOTS`/`PACKAGE_ROOTS_EXCLUDE` inventories of
  `clotho/inventory.mjs` and the contract
  `clotho/memory/CONTRACTS/package-roots.json` (only after Phase B activation).
