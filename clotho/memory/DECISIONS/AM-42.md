  both Iliad councils explicitly HELD for a separate Eye ruling: `lachesis` and
  `atropos` are CONSCIOUSLY ENROLLED as spine packages. This AMENDS AM-40's
  closed set (AM-40's bytes are never rewritten; its five-package ruling remains
  the true record of what was ruled then). The closed membership is now
  machine-bound to this document — verify-contracts parses the single fenced
  JSON block below and requires the contract arrays to deep-equal it, so
  neither the roots NOR the exclusions can drift again without a new
  hash-pinned ruling:

```json
{
  "package_roots": ["atropos", "breakout", "build-gate", "clotho", "connectors/ai-peer-mcp", "lachesis", "merkle-dag"],
  "package_roots_exclude": ["ai-forge", "ai-native-memory", "demo", "forge", "narcissus/flagship", "saas-forge"]
```

  council-authorized (`authz-lachesis-1`, `authz-atropos-1`), but each council
  scoped the enrollment-flip itself as "HELD for a separate Eye ruling (not
  authorized by this council)", and no ruling record existed while the code,
  contract, and committed self-weave already lived the seven-root state.
  Honest framing: enrollment commit 24266a8 invoked a general Eye directive
  ("get every module operating on the workflow"), so this ruling ratifies
  retroactively rather than blessing a rogue act — but a general directive is
  not an enrollment ruling, and the held item stays held until this record.
  The exclusion side had drifted the same way three times without any
  amendment record (`ai-native-memory`, `narcissus/flagship`, `demo` were each
  added exclude-side), which is why this ruling binds BOTH arrays.

  `clotho/inventory.mjs` and the contract
  `clotho/memory/CONTRACTS/package-roots.json`.
